profiles) and `isis1_retrieval` (every usable ISIS-1 profile); script

M2 includes measured profiles: "we want as close to truth as possible and we
also want to show where current data and models are lacking as the impetus for

## Records and scoring

7,790 ISIS-1 records: scaling quality index 4 to 9 (0 best, 10 worst; 4 to 10
in these files), spacecraft at 500-1100 km, at least 6 points.  Each is flown
at its own position and epoch (`MeasuredTopside`: the profile above its lowest
scaled height, an alpha-Chapman cap below), once with clutter on and once off.

SCI-05 (rms fractional N_e error; threshold 0.15, goal 0.10) and SCI-07 (rms
height error at matched density; threshold 20 km, goal 10 km) are scored as
the assessment tier scores them, over two scopes: the **measured span** (truth
and retrieval both cut to the heights ISIS-1 scaled; the gate) and the **full
profile** (from the truth peak up, so including the Chapman cap).

## Why the P4 chain fails on measured profiles

Diagnosed on 36 records, then on 16 with every column printed against the
truth echoes:

1. **The X start was taken for f_N.**  Under a shallow topside gradient (an
   H+ topside, night, high latitude, a spacecraft near 1000 km) the O trace
   starts hundreds of kilometres out, because the group retardation over the
   last few kilometres below the spacecraft grows from zero within one
   frequency step: for an exponential topside of scale height H the virtual
   range a step above the cut-off is, field-free, about 2H artanh(sqrt(1 - (f_N/f)^2)),
   some 0.4 H at 2.5 % above f_N, 250 km for H = 600 km.  The X trace starts
   near range.  P4's prior looked for the lowest near-range echo a trace
   follows, found the X start, and every search above it was shifted: the O
   path started on X.
2. **L4 never used the tracker's estimate.**  It started from the lowest
   detected O frequency, which is above f_N by up to a step plus however many
   columns were missed, so the starting density reads high and the whole
   profile inherits it.  (The docstring in `l4_retrieval/base.py` had the sign
   of this bias wrong; corrected.)
3. **Measured O traces are not monotone.**  They rise hundreds of kilometres
   per column at the start and fall back tens of kilometres at ledges; the
   tracker's path is monotone (4 km down tolerance) and slope-limited, so it
   keeps the best monotone segment.  Not fixed (see cusp tracking below).

## The fixes, developed on a random 400 records

Share of runs at threshold or better over the measured span, SCI-05 / SCI-07,
on the same 400 records (seed 20261105), with the Chapman campaign checked
for each:

| Variant | ISIS-1 clutter on | ISIS-1 clutter off | Chapman |
|---|---|---|---|
| P4 chain (baseline) | 74.5 / 64.5 % | 73.3 / 72.8 % | as P4 |
| L4 starts from the tracker's estimate only | 82.0 / 84.0 % | 74.0 / 73.5 % | all goal; SCI-07 2.5-3.0 km median |
| **+ the trace-floor prior (default)** | **84.8 / 87.8 %** | **84.5 / 90.3 %** | **all goal; labelling as P4** |
| + stop at loss | 86.3 / 86.8 % | 88.0 / 90.0 % | all goal; O completeness 0.67 -> 0.57 (on), 0.95 -> 0.69 (off) |
| + cusp tracking (400 km rises at the steep ends) | 84.8 / 87.8 % | 84.3 / 90.5 % | unchanged |

* **The starting value** (`start_policy: onboard`): L4 starts from the
  tracker's f_N where it is below the lowest detected frequency.  With clutter
  on the resonances give f_N and this alone does most of the work; with clutter
  off there is no resonance and it needs the trace floor.
* **The trace-floor prior** (`fn_prior: trace_floor`): f_N is bounded by the
  lowest frequency at which any persistent trace starts, at any range (no echo
  returns below the local plasma frequency), less half a step.  The O path may
  start far out in the first columns above it, at a reduced cost, and the O
  search is not cut off below it, since a weak start may have been missed.  A
  first version also let X start far out and cut O off below the floor; on 9
  records that let X take the O trace or lost a weak start, and both were
  removed.
* **Stop at loss** (`stop_at_loss`, off): end the O trace where its samples sit
  on the predicted X trace for two columns, or turn track-only.  It helps
  SCI-05 on ISIS-1 by 1.5 to 3.5 points but cuts valid samples on the Chapman
  profiles (completeness 0.95 to 0.69 with clutter off), so the profile ends
  higher.  Off by default; a coverage-for-error trade for Andrew.
* **Cusp tracking** (`cusp_up_cap_m`, off): no measurable gain.  The cusps
  that matter jump by hundreds to a thousand kilometres in a step, and beyond
  the 1050 km search range.

## The M2 result (`data/reference/wp4_m2_*`, commit 847f4c5)

The default chain (trace-floor prior, onboard starting value) over every
record.  A first full run at 6e4fc9b passed the gate, but with clutter on 64
records that the P4 chain had right now failed, mostly at high f_N: a
gyroharmonic ringing one column off its nominal frequency (the exclusion
tolerance was half a step) was taken for f_N, which P4 never used but L4 now
starts from.  At 847f4c5 the resonance is the highest candidate at or below
the trace floor (stepped down to its partner when it is f_UH), and the floor
search leaves out columns within a step of a harmonic.

## Where the models and the data fall short (`data/reference/wp4_m2_models`)

**IRI-2020 against ISIS-1.**  PHaRLAP's IRI-2020 at each record's time and
place, on the epoch's own indices, foF2 storm model on, scored against the
measured profile over the measured span on the same metrics and limits:

| Topside | SCI-05 metric median (p90) | at threshold or better | SCI-07 metric km median (p90) | at threshold or better | f_N at the spacecraft, dB median [p5, p95] |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NeQuick (IRI-2020 default) | 0.42 (1.24) | 12 % | 70 (162) | 8 % | +0.6 [-4.1, +4.8] |
| IRI-2001 | 0.52 (2.47) | 6 % | 86 (225) | 4 % | +2.4 [-2.9, +9.2] |

The model is worst where the chain is weakest: under 1.5 MHz at the spacecraft
NeQuick's median error is 0.65 and 3 % of records are at threshold, IRI-2001
reads the density there 6.4 dB high.  These are climatological profiles
compared with individual soundings, which is the comparison a user without a
sounder is left with.  The plasma-frequency ratio agrees with WP0 Phase 5
(NeQuick +0.3 to +0.7 dB at 900-1400 km).
